Housing, on the backing

Turning

Moving the housing carries the dial with it (fixed offset) — try dragging horizontal/vertical to opposite corners of the panel: THIS is the actual thing being demonstrated here, since the real UI will place this dial+housing pair differently on every screen. Each turn is a fixed 45° over a locked 0.25s. The dial's own offset/size within the housing, and the turn duration, are all settled and baked into dialProto.js now — this page only exposes what still varies screen to screen. The window above the dial hole is still just a cut-out for now; the white paper + changing values come later.
